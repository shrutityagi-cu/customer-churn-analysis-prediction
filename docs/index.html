05 / SQL INTELLIGENCE

From raw records to business decisions.

SQL analysis was used to investigate customer segments and identify retention opportunities.

churn_analysis.sql 
-- Identify high-risk customers

SELECT
    customerID,
    Contract,
    tenure,
    MonthlyCharges,
    InternetService,
    PaymentMethod,
    Churn

FROM customers

WHERE
    Contract = 'Month-to-month'
    AND tenure <= 12
    AND MonthlyCharges > 70;
